About

We are a creative film & video production collective crafting authentic visual narratives for artists, brands, and organizations.

Photo by Tandem X Visuals

Our Philosophy

At Drift, we believe in the power of authentic visual storytelling. We work collaboratively with our clients to create cinematic narratives that resonate with audiences and remain true to their vision.

Our approach combines technical excellence with artistic sensitivity, allowing us to capture moments that evoke emotion and create lasting impressions.

Core Values

  • — Authenticity in every frame
  • — Collaborative creative process
  • — Technical precision with artistic vision
  • — Sustainable production practices

Commercial Production

We produce high-impact commercials for brands worldwide. From ideation to delivery, our team crafts visually stunning campaigns that drive results.

  • Brand Films for Nike, Apple, and Samsung
  • Product Launches for Tesla, Sony, and Adidas
  • Social Media Spots for Coca-Cola, Airbnb, and Uber
  • TV Commercials for BMW, Mercedes-Benz, and Ford
  • Digital Ads for Google, Amazon, and Facebook

Recent campaign: "Drive the Future" for BMW

Music & Entertainment

We create music videos, live sessions, and documentaries for top artists and labels.

  • Music Videos for Billie Eilish, The Weeknd, and BTS
  • Live Concert Films for Coldplay, Ed Sheeran, and Adele
  • Tour Documentaries for Taylor Swift, Drake, and Dua Lipa
  • Behind-the-Scenes for Universal Music, Sony Music
  • Visualizers for Spotify and Apple Music

Recent project: "Starlight" Live Session for Dua Lipa

Fashion & Editorial

Our team produces high-end fashion films, lookbooks, and editorial content for global brands and magazines.

  • Fashion Films for Vogue, GQ, and Harper's Bazaar
  • Lookbooks for Gucci, Prada, and Louis Vuitton
  • Editorial Shoots for Elle, Dazed, and i-D
  • Runway Coverage for Paris, Milan, and NY Fashion Weeks
  • Designer Interviews and Profiles

Recent editorial: "Urban Elegance" for Vogue Paris

Documentary

We tell real stories with depth and authenticity, from social issues to cultural explorations.

  • Short Documentaries for BBC, Netflix, and Vice
  • Feature Docs for National Geographic, Discovery
  • Cultural Series for The Guardian, Al Jazeera
  • Profile Films for TED, Wired, and TIME
  • Impact Stories for NGOs and Charities

Award-winning: "Voices Unheard" for BBC

Art & Installation

Experimental films and immersive installations for galleries, museums, and artists.

  • Gallery Installations for Tate Modern, MoMA, and LACMA
  • Projection Mapping for Art Basel, Venice Biennale
  • Experimental Shorts for Film Festivals
  • Artist Collaborations with Ai Weiwei, Yayoi Kusama
  • Multi-screen Experiences for Museums

Recent: "Light Forms" at Tate Modern

Post-Production

Editing, color grading, VFX, and sound design for film, TV, and digital content.

  • Editing for Netflix Originals, HBO, and Amazon Prime
  • Color Grading for Feature Films and Commercials
  • Visual Effects for Marvel, Disney, and Warner Bros
  • Sound Design for Documentaries and Music Videos
  • Delivery for Cinema, TV, and Web

Recent: "Neon Dreams" color grade for Amazon Studios
